Our Vision

A community pursuing
spiritual maturity together

The Maturity Project is a fellowship of 40 believers committed to growing deeper in the Word of God. We believe that spiritual maturity isn't a destination — it's a journey we walk together, season by season, study by study.

Through structured Bible study, Godly commissions, and mutual accountability, we're becoming who God created us to be.

The Path

Stages of Growth

01

Foundation

Rooted in the Word. Building the habits of daily study, prayer, and fellowship.

02

Formation

Shaped by truth. Developing understanding and letting Scripture transform the mind.

03

Commission

Sent with purpose. Discovering and walking in your God-given assignment.

04

Multiplication

Bearing fruit. Pouring into others and extending the Kingdom through service.

Godly Commissions

Called to Serve

Each commission is an extension of our purpose — equipping members to serve the body and the world with their unique gifts.

🕊️

Prayer & Intercession

Standing in the gap for the fellowship, our families, and our communities through consistent, fervent prayer.

📖

Teaching & Discipleship

Facilitating Bible studies, mentoring new believers, and ensuring sound doctrine flows through the fellowship.

🤝

Outreach & Evangelism

Extending the love of Christ beyond our walls — community service, campus ministry, and missions.

🎵

Worship & Creative Arts

Leading the fellowship into God's presence through music, spoken word, and creative expression.

💛

Welfare & Care

Meeting practical needs, checking on members, and creating a culture of genuine love and support.

📋

Administration & Media

Keeping the fellowship organized, managing communications, and documenting our journey.
